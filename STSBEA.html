An immersive recreation of Hogsmeade village, complete with snow-dusted rooftops, Ollivanders wand shop, and Hogwarts castle looming over everything. Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey is a motion-simulator dark ride that takes guests soaring over the Hogwarts grounds, while Flight of the Hippogriff is a gentler family coaster. The detail in the shopfronts and soundscape make this one of the most atmospheric areas to simply walk through, even without riding anything.
A dense, jungle-like zone that recreates the world of the Jurassic Park films. The Flying Dinosaur is one of the park's most intense rides — an inverted coaster with steep drops — while Jurassic Park: The Ride is a boat ride that builds tension before a big splashdown finale. The area also runs several short dinosaur encounter shows at different points, like Jurassic World Raptor Alert and Baby Dino Adventure, so it's worth checking the show schedule if you want to catch them.
A vividly colorful, densely designed area built around the Mario games, where guests can wear a Power-Up Band to interact with question blocks and bosses throughout the land. Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge is an augmented-reality ride putting guests behind the wheel in a kart race, while Yoshi's Adventure is a gentler ride suited to younger visitors. Since 2024, the area has expanded to include Donkey Kong Country, home to the Mine Cart Madness coaster — a fast-paced ride designed to feel like it's leaping across broken tracks. This whole zone tends to be the most crowded in the park, and often uses timed-entry tickets on busy days
This is the park's dedicated family zone, aimed at younger children and split into two themed sections: Snoopy Studio, based on the Peanuts comic strip, and Hello Kitty's Fashion Avenue. Rides here are gentle — think spinning cupcakes and a mild Snoopy-themed coaster — and character meet-and-greets and stage shows run multiple times a day. It's worth noting that the area's third section, Sesame Street Fun Zone, permanently closed in 2026, so the space is now smaller than it used to be.
This is the park's grand entrance, styled after the golden age of American cinema with palm-lined streets and film-studio facades. It's the hub you return to for the daily parade and night shows on the lagoon. The star attraction is Hollywood Dream — The Ride, a coaster known for being remarkably smooth despite its speed, and famous for letting riders choose their own background music (locals swear by "Osaka Lover"). Nearby, Space Fantasy: The Ride offers a fast indoor coaster experience that periodically collaborates with popular anime themes. Hollywood is also packed with shops and dining, making it a natural start and end point for a day at the park.
